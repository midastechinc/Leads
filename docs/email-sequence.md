# Midas Tech Email Outreach Sequence

These are the emails the lead tracker drafts from **Lead details → Email drafts**. Emails 1 and 2 change by industry, which is detected from the lead's title, industry and company. Emails 3 and 4 are the same for everyone.

`{{first}}` is the contact's first name ("there" if unknown), `{{firm}}` is the company, `{{season}}` is the current season. The examples below use "Jane" and a sample company.

Suggested timing: Email 1 → Email 2 after 5–7 days → Email 3 a week later → Email 4 a week after that.
